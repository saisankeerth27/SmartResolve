# Data Usage and Throttling Policies

## Data Plan Tiers

### Basic Plan

Includes 5 GB of high-speed data per month. After the data limit is reached, data speeds are reduced to 128 Kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle. The reduced speed applies to all data usage. The data limit resets at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

### Standard Plan

Includes 15 GB of high-speed data per month. After the data limit is reached, data speeds are reduced to 256 Kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle. The reduced speed applies to all data usage. The data limit resets at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

### Premium Plan

Includes 50 GB of high-speed data per month. After the data limit is reached, data speeds are reduced to 512 Kbps for the remainder of the billing cycle. The reduced speed applies to all data usage. The data limit resets at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

### Unlimited Plan

Includes unlimited high-speed data with no throttling. Subject to fair use policy. May be deprioritized during network congestion after 100 GB of usage. Deprioritization is temporary and based on network conditions.

## Throttling Mechanism

### Speed Reduction

When a customer reaches their data limit, speed is reduced to the plan's throttled rate. The throttled rate is applied to all data traffic. No data is blocked, only slowed. The customer remains connected at the reduced speed.

### Data Usage Tracking

Data usage is tracked in real-time. Usage is recorded in 1 KB increments. The customer can check their current usage via the mobile app or customer portal. Usage updates may have a delay of up to 15 minutes.

### Throttle Removal

Speed restrictions are removed at the start of the next billing cycle. No action is required by the customer. The transition is automatic. If the customer upgrades their plan mid-cycle, the new plan's data limit applies immediately.

## Overage Charges

### Pay-Per-Use Overage

Customers on plans with data limits can opt for pay-per-use overage. Overage is charged at ₹99 per additional GB. Overage charges are capped at ₹499 per billing cycle. The cap does not include the base plan charge.

### Data Top-Up

Customers can purchase additional high-speed data at any time. Data top-up is available in 1 GB increments. Each 1 GB top-up costs ₹99. Top-up data does not roll over to the next billing cycle.

## Data Usage Monitoring

### Mobile App

Customers can check data usage through the SmartConnect mobile app. Real-time usage is displayed on the home screen. Historical usage charts show daily and monthly trends. Alerts can be configured for usage thresholds.

### Customer Portal

The web-based customer portal provides detailed usage information. Usage can be broken down by application type. Daily usage summaries are available. Usage alerts can be configured via email or SMS.

### Usage Alerts

Automatic alerts are sent at 50%, 75%, 90%, and 100% of the data limit. Alerts are sent via SMS and push notification. Additional custom alerts can be configured. Alerts are free and do not count against data usage.

## Enterprise Plans

### Dedicated Bandwidth

Enterprise plans include dedicated bandwidth with guaranteed minimum speeds. No throttling is applied to enterprise plans. Bandwidth is reserved for the customer's exclusive use. Upgrades and downgrades are managed through the account manager.

### Burst Capacity

Enterprise plans include burst capacity above the guaranteed bandwidth. Burst capacity is available during off-peak hours. Peak hours are defined as 8 AM to 8 PM local time. Burst capacity is not guaranteed and depends on network availability.
