# Billing Cycle Management

## Overview

This document describes how SmartConnect manages billing cycles, charges, and credits for customer accounts.

## Billing Cycle Structure

### Monthly Billing

Most plans are billed monthly on the anniversary of the service activation date. The billing cycle day is set at activation and remains consistent each month. Charges are prorated for the first and last month of service.

### Billing Cycle Day

The billing cycle day (BCD) is the day of the month when the bill is generated. This is determined by the customer's activation date. For example, if activated on the 15th, the BCD is the 15th of each month. The customer can request a BCD change, which may result in a short or long billing cycle.

### Bill Generation

Bills are generated on the BCD and include all charges for the billing period. Charges include recurring plan fees, usage charges, taxes, and fees. Credits and adjustments are applied before the final amount is calculated.

## Prorated Charges

### First Month Proration

When a customer activates mid-cycle, the first month is prorated. The proration is based on the number of days remaining in the billing cycle. For example, if activated on the 20th and the cycle ends on the 30th, the customer is charged for 11 days.

### Last Month Proration

When a customer deactivates mid-cycle, the final month is prorated. The proration is based on the number of days the service was active in the billing cycle. Any unused portion of the billing cycle is credited.

### Plan Change Proration

When a customer changes plans mid-cycle, charges are prorated. The old plan is prorated for the days it was active. The new plan is prorated for the remaining days. The difference is charged or credited accordingly.

## Credits and Adjustments

### Service Credits

Credits for service disruptions or quality issues. Must be approved by a supervisor. Applied to the customer's next bill. Documented with a reason code and description.

### Goodwill Credits

Credits offered as a gesture of goodwill. Typically for customer retention or complaint resolution. Must be approved by a supervisor. Applied to the customer's next bill.

### Promotional Credits

Credits from promotional offers or discounts. Applied automatically based on the promotion terms. May be recurring or one-time. Expire at the end of the promotional period.

## Late Payment and Non-Payment

### Late Payment Fee

A late payment fee is charged if payment is not received by the due date. The fee is a percentage of the outstanding balance. The fee is capped at a maximum amount per billing cycle. Customers are notified before the late fee is applied.

### Grace Period

A grace period of 10 days is provided after the bill due date. No late fees are charged during the grace period. Service is not affected during the grace period. After the grace period, late fees are applied and service may be suspended.

### Non-Payment Suspension

If payment is not received within 30 days of the due date, service may be suspended. The customer is notified before suspension. All services including voice, data, and messaging are suspended. The customer must pay the outstanding balance plus any reconnection fees to restore service.

## Refund Policy

### Voluntary Cancellation Refund

Customers who cancel within the first 14 days receive a full refund minus any usage charges. Customers who cancel after 14 days receive a prorated refund for the unused portion of the current billing cycle. Any device subsidies or promotional balances become due immediately upon cancellation.

### Involuntary Termination Refund

Customers whose service is terminated due to non-payment do not receive a refund. Any device financing balances become due immediately. The account may be sent to collections if the balance remains unpaid.
