# Invoice Dispute Procedures

## Overview

This document describes how SmartConnect handles disputes regarding invoice charges, billing errors, and service credits.

## Types of Disputes

### Billing Error

The customer believes there is an error on their invoice. Examples include incorrect charges, missing credits, wrong plan pricing, or duplicate charges. Billing errors must be investigated and resolved within 30 days of the dispute.

### Service Quality

The customer experienced service quality issues and requests a credit. Examples include dropped calls, slow data, or service outages. Service quality credits require verification of the reported issue.

### Unauthorized Charges

The customer did not authorize or recognize a charge on their invoice. Examples include third-party charges, premium SMS, or subscription services. Unauthorized charges require investigation and may involve third-party coordination.

### Contract Dispute

The customer disagrees with contract terms or early termination fees. Examples include device financing, promotional commitments, or service agreements. Contract disputes are reviewed by the customer relations team.

## Dispute Resolution Process

### Step 1: Customer Contact

The customer contacts SmartConnect to initiate a dispute. The dispute can be initiated via phone, email, chat, or in-store visit. The customer provides their account number, invoice number, and description of the dispute. A dispute case number is generated and provided to the customer.

### Step 2: Initial Review

The customer service representative reviews the dispute during the initial contact. If the dispute can be resolved immediately, the representative applies the appropriate credit or adjustment. If the dispute requires investigation, it is escalated to the billing investigation team.

### Step 3: Investigation

The billing investigation team reviews all relevant information. This includes account history, usage records, service logs, and any supporting documentation provided by the customer. The investigation is completed within 10 business days for standard disputes and 30 business days for complex disputes.

### Step 4: Resolution

The investigation team determines the appropriate resolution. If the dispute is valid, a credit or adjustment is applied to the customer's account. If the dispute is not valid, the customer is notified with an explanation. The customer may appeal the decision if they disagree with the outcome.

### Step 5: Customer Notification

The customer is notified of the resolution via their preferred communication method. The notification includes a summary of the investigation, the determination, and any credits or adjustments applied. The customer's invoice is updated to reflect the resolution.

## Credit Limits

### Automatic Credits

Credits below $25 can be applied by customer service representatives without supervisor approval. Credits between $25 and $100 require supervisor approval. Credits above $100 require management approval. All credits are documented with a reason code.

### Recurring Credits

Recurring credits for ongoing issues are limited to 3 billing cycles. After 3 cycles, the issue must be escalated for permanent resolution. Recurring credits must be reviewed monthly to ensure they are still appropriate.

## Time Limits

### Dispute Filing Deadline

Customers must file disputes within 60 days of the invoice date. Disputes filed after 60 days may not be eligible for credit. Exceptions are made for circumstances beyond the customer's control.

### Resolution Deadline

Standard disputes must be resolved within 30 business days. Complex disputes involving third parties may take up to 60 business days. Customers are notified of any delays and provided with an updated timeline.

### Appeal Deadline

Customers must file appeals within 30 days of the initial resolution. Appeals are reviewed by a different team than the initial investigation. The appeal decision is final and binding.
