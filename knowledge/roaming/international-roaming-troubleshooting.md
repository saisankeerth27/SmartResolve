# International Roaming Troubleshooting Guide

## Pre-Travel Checklist

### Verify Account Status

Confirm the customer's account is in good standing with no past due balance. Verify the line is active and not suspended. Confirm the customer has an active international roaming plan or understands pay-per-use rates.

### Device Preparation

Ensure the device is unlocked if it was purchased with a carrier lock. Verify the device supports the frequency bands used in the destination country. Update device software to the latest version. Back up important data before traveling.

### Settings Configuration

Enable data roaming in device settings. Verify APN settings are correct for international use. Enable Wi-Fi calling as a backup option. Set up the SmartConnect mobile app for account management while abroad.

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Device Cannot Find Network

If the device cannot find any network in the destination country: verify the device is not in airplane mode, check that data roaming is enabled in device settings, restart the device and wait 5 minutes for network search, manually select a network operator in device settings, and verify the destination country is covered by SmartConnect roaming partners.

### Network Registered but No Service

If the device shows a network name but cannot make calls or use data: verify roaming is enabled on the SmartConnect account, check that the international roaming plan is activated, verify the device APN settings are correct, try toggling airplane mode on and off, and contact SmartConnect support for account verification.

### Data Not Working

If data does not work while roaming: verify mobile data is enabled in device settings, confirm data roaming is turned on, check that the roaming plan includes data, verify APN settings are configured for international use, try connecting to Wi-Fi and using the SmartConnect app to troubleshoot, and restart the device after making any setting changes.

### MMS Not Working

MMS may not be available in all roaming destinations. Verify the destination country supports MMS roaming. Check that mobile data is working. Verify MMS APN settings are correct. Some roaming partners do not support MMS, which is a known limitation.

### Unexpected Charges

If a customer receives unexpected charges: review the detailed usage in the SmartConnect app or portal, verify the roaming plan was activated before travel, check if the usage was domestic or international roaming, review the per-use rates for the destination country, and initiate a billing dispute if the charges are incorrect.

## Advanced Troubleshooting

### SIM Card Issues While Abroad

If the SIM card is not recognized: remove and reinsert the SIM card, try the SIM in a different device if available, check for physical damage to the SIM card, contact SmartConnect for SIM replacement options while abroad.

### Device Compatibility Issues

If the device does not work on roaming networks: verify the device supports the required frequency bands, check if the device is carrier-locked, confirm the device software is up to date, try a different device if available.

## Emergency Roaming

### Emergency Calls

Emergency calls (911, 112, 999) can be made even without roaming activation. The device will connect to any available network for emergency calls. Emergency calls may be routed to the nearest emergency center regardless of the network used.

### Emergency Contacts

If a customer needs emergency assistance while abroad: they can call SmartConnect customer service from any phone, they can visit a SmartConnect partner store in the destination country, they can use the SmartConnect app over Wi-Fi for support, and the emergency support line is available 24/7.
