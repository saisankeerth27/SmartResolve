# Roaming Policies and Procedures

## Overview

SmartConnect provides domestic and international roaming services to customers traveling outside their home coverage area. This document describes the policies, procedures, and troubleshooting for roaming services.

## Domestic Roaming

### What is Domestic Roaming

Domestic roaming occurs when a SmartConnect customer connects to another carrier's network within the country. This happens when the customer is outside SmartConnect's coverage area but within a roaming partner's coverage area. Roaming is automatic and requires no customer action.

### Domestic Roaming Charges

Domestic roaming is included at no additional cost for all postpaid plans. Data usage while roaming counts toward the customer's plan data limit. Voice and SMS usage while roaming is included in the plan allowances. There are no separate roaming fees for domestic roaming.

### Domestic Roaming Troubleshooting

If a customer cannot connect to a roaming network: verify roaming is enabled on the account, check that the customer is outside the SmartConnect coverage area, verify the device supports the roaming partner's frequency bands, and restart the device to force network reselection.

## International Roaming

### International Roaming Plans

SmartConnect offers international roaming plans for customers traveling abroad. Plans are available in daily, weekly, and monthly options. Each plan includes a set amount of data, voice minutes, and SMS. Plans can be activated before departure or while abroad.

### International Roaming Charges

Without an international roaming plan, pay-per-use rates apply. Pay-per-use data is charged at $2.00 per MB. Pay-per-use voice calls are charged at $2.00 per minute for outgoing calls. Incoming calls are charged at $0.50 per minute. SMS is charged at $0.10 per message.

### International Roaming Activation

Customers must request international roaming activation before traveling. Activation can be done via the mobile app, customer portal, or by contacting customer service. A security deposit may be required for high-risk destinations. Activation takes effect within 24 hours.

## Roaming Partner Management

### Partner Selection

The device automatically selects the best available roaming partner based on signal strength and quality. SmartConnect maintains roaming agreements with major carriers in each country. The customer does not need to manually select a network.

### Partner Coverage

Roaming partner coverage may differ from the home network. The customer may experience different speeds and quality depending on the roaming partner. Coverage maps for roaming partners are available on the SmartConnect website.

## Roaming Troubleshooting

### No Service While Roaming

Verify roaming is enabled on the account. Check that the device has data roaming enabled in settings. Restart the device to force network reselection. If no service persists, manually select a network operator. Contact support if the issue continues.

### Slow Data While Roaming

Roaming speeds may be slower than the home network. This is normal and depends on the roaming partner's network. Try moving to a different location for better signal. Disable background data usage to conserve roaming data. Consider activating an international roaming plan for better speeds.

### High Roaming Charges

If a customer receives unexpected roaming charges: review the customer's roaming usage, check if roaming plans were activated, verify if the usage was domestic or international, and apply credits if appropriate per the billing dispute policy.
