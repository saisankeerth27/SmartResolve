# Enterprise Support Operations

## Overview

This document describes the specialized support operations for SmartConnect enterprise customers, including dedicated support teams, priority handling, and proactive monitoring.

## Enterprise Support Structure

### Dedicated Account Management

Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated account manager. The account manager is the primary point of contact for all service-related matters. The account manager conducts regular business reviews and coordinates service improvements. Account manager contact information is provided in the service agreement.

### Priority Support Queue

Enterprise customer inquiries are routed to a priority support queue. The priority queue has shorter wait times and more experienced representatives. Enterprise customers bypass the standard IVR (Interactive Voice Response) system. Direct dial numbers are provided for enterprise support.

### Technical Account Manager

Technical Account Managers (TAMs) provide advanced technical support for enterprise customers. TAMs have deep knowledge of the customer's network configuration and requirements. TAMs proactively monitor the customer's service and address potential issues. TAMs are available for architecture consultations and planning.

## Proactive Monitoring

### 24/7 Monitoring

Enterprise customer connections are monitored 24/7 by the Network Operations Center (NOC). Monitoring includes availability, performance, and security metrics. Anomalies are detected and investigated automatically. Proactive alerts are sent to the customer's designated contacts.

### Health Checks

Regular health checks are performed on enterprise customer connections. Health checks include bandwidth verification, latency testing, and configuration review. Health check results are included in monthly reports. Issues identified during health checks are resolved proactively.

### Capacity Planning

Enterprise customer usage patterns are analyzed for capacity planning. The account manager provides capacity recommendations based on usage trends. Capacity upgrades are recommended before the customer experiences performance issues. Capacity planning is included in quarterly business reviews.

## Incident Management for Enterprise

### Dedicated Incident Response

Enterprise customer incidents receive dedicated response from senior engineers. Incident bridges are established for P1 incidents. The customer's designated contacts are notified immediately. Regular status updates are provided during incident resolution.

### Post-Incident Review

All P1 and P2 incidents affecting enterprise customers require a post-incident review. The review includes a detailed timeline, root cause analysis, and preventive recommendations. The review report is shared with the customer within 5 business days. Action items from the review are tracked to completion.

### Communication Protocol

Enterprise customers receive proactive communication during service incidents. Initial notification within 15 minutes of detection. Status updates every 30 minutes during active incidents. Resolution notification with summary and next steps. Follow-up communication within 24 hours with detailed report.

## Custom Solutions

### Network Design

Enterprise customers can request custom network designs. Custom designs are developed by the Solutions Engineering team. Designs include detailed architecture, capacity planning, and implementation timelines. Custom designs are reviewed and approved by the customer before implementation.

### Integration Services

SmartConnect provides integration services for enterprise customers. Integration services include API connectivity, VPN configuration, and custom routing. Integration services are scoped and priced based on complexity. Integration projects include project management and post-implementation support.

### Managed Services

Enterprise customers can subscribe to managed services for network operations. Managed services include monitoring, configuration management, and incident response. Managed services are tiered based on the level of support required. Managed services SLAs are specified in the service agreement.

## Enterprise Billing

### Custom Invoicing

Enterprise customers receive custom invoices tailored to their requirements. Custom invoices can include cost center allocation, department breakdowns, and project-based billing. Invoice formats include PDF, CSV, and EDI. Invoice delivery schedules are flexible.

### Volume Discounts

Enterprise customers receive volume discounts based on total service commitment. Discount tiers are: 5-10 lines: 5% discount, 11-25 lines: 10% discount, 26-50 lines: 15% discount, 51+ lines: 20% discount. Discounts apply to all recurring service charges.

### Payment Terms

Standard payment terms for enterprise customers: Net 30 days from invoice date. Extended payment terms may be available for large commitments. Early payment discounts may be available for annual prepayment. Payment methods include ACH, wire transfer, and corporate credit card.
