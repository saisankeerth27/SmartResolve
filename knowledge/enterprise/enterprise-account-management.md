# Enterprise Account Management

## Overview

This document describes how SmartConnect manages enterprise customer accounts, including onboarding, ongoing management, and account reviews.

## Enterprise Account Lifecycle

### Onboarding

Enterprise account onboarding begins with a kickoff meeting between the customer's team and SmartConnect's account team. The onboarding process includes: requirements gathering, solution design, implementation planning, service activation, and post-activation verification. Onboarding is typically completed within 30 days of contract signing.

### Ongoing Management

Enterprise accounts are managed through regular business reviews and proactive communication. The account manager conducts monthly check-ins with the customer's primary contacts. Quarterly business reviews include SLA performance analysis and optimization recommendations. Annual reviews include contract review and strategic planning.

### Renewal

Enterprise account renewals begin 90 days before the contract end date. The account manager initiates renewal discussions with the customer. Renewal includes service review, pricing update, and contract terms negotiation. Renewal offers are prepared and presented 60 days before contract end.

## Account Management Team

### Account Manager

The account manager is the primary point of contact for the enterprise customer. Responsibilities include: service oversight, issue escalation, business reviews, and contract management. The account manager is assigned at the beginning of the relationship and remains consistent.

### Technical Account Manager (TAM)

The TAM provides technical expertise for the enterprise customer. Responsibilities include: technical consulting, architecture review, capacity planning, and incident management. The TAM works closely with the account manager and the customer's technical team.

### Customer Success Manager

The Customer Success Manager focuses on maximizing customer value from SmartConnect services. Responsibilities include: adoption tracking, value realization, and optimization recommendations. The Customer Success Manager is available for Platinum and Gold tier customers.

## Business Reviews

### Monthly Check-In

The account manager conducts monthly check-ins with the customer's primary contacts. The check-in includes: service performance summary, open issue review, upcoming changes, and any customer concerns. The check-in is documented and shared with the customer.

### Quarterly Business Review

Quarterly business reviews include comprehensive SLA performance analysis, usage trends and capacity review, optimization recommendations, and strategic alignment discussion. The review is prepared by the account team and presented to the customer's leadership.

### Annual Review

Annual reviews include: contract review and renewal planning, service portfolio review, strategic roadmap alignment, and executive relationship building. The review involves both organizations' leadership teams.

## Account Health Monitoring

### Health Score

Each enterprise account is assigned a health score based on multiple factors: SLA performance, support ticket trends, usage growth, and relationship sentiment. The health score is updated monthly and reviewed by the account team.

### Risk Indicators

The following indicators trigger account health reviews: SLA breaches exceeding 2 per quarter, support ticket volume increasing more than 20% quarter-over-quarter, usage declining more than 10% quarter-over-quarter, or negative sentiment detected in business reviews.

### Intervention

When risk indicators are detected, the account team develops an intervention plan. The plan may include: additional resources, service enhancements, pricing adjustments, or executive engagement. The intervention plan is implemented and monitored for effectiveness.

## Communication Channels

### Primary Contact

The account manager is the primary contact for all non-urgent communications. The account manager is available during business hours and responds within 4 hours. For urgent matters, the account manager can be reached via mobile phone.

### Technical Contact

The TAM is the primary contact for technical inquiries and incidents. The TAM is available during business hours and on-call for P1 incidents. Technical inquiries are responded to within 2 hours.

### Executive Contact

Executive sponsors from both organizations maintain a strategic relationship. Executive contact is reserved for significant issues, strategic discussions, and contract negotiations. Executive contact is coordinated through the account manager.

## Account Documentation

### Service Agreement

The service agreement defines the terms and conditions of the enterprise service. The agreement includes: service description, SLA guarantees, pricing, and contract terms. The agreement is maintained by the account manager and shared with the customer.

### Network Design Document

The network design document describes the technical architecture of the enterprise service. The document includes: network topology, configuration details, capacity specifications, and integration requirements. The document is maintained by the TAM.

### Meeting Minutes

All business reviews and significant meetings are documented. Meeting minutes include: attendees, discussion topics, decisions made, and action items. Meeting minutes are shared with all attendees within 2 business days. Action items are tracked to completion.
