# Service Activation Procedures

## Overview

This document describes the procedures for activating new services and modifying existing services for SmartConnect customers.

## New Line Activation

### Pre-Activation Checks

Verify customer identity and account status. Confirm the customer has a valid IMEI for the device they will use. Check that the desired phone number is available. Verify the selected plan is available for the customer's segment. Confirm the customer understands the service terms and pricing.

### Activation Steps

1. Create or locate the customer account in the billing system.
2. Select the desired plan and add it to the order.
3. Generate a new SIM card assignment or eSIM profile.
4. Associate the SIM with the customer account and selected plan.
5. Provision network access for the new subscription.
6. Configure the customer's device with the correct APN settings.
7. Verify service is working by placing a test call and using data.
8. Send welcome SMS and email with account details.

### Post-Activation Verification

Make a test call to verify voice service. Send a test SMS to verify messaging. Run a speed test to verify data service. Verify the customer can access their account portal. Confirm billing is set up correctly.

## Plan Change Procedures

### Upgrade

Verify the customer's eligibility for the new plan. Check for any contract commitments that may be affected. Calculate any prorated charges for the current billing cycle. Apply the new plan effective immediately or at next billing cycle. Notify the customer of the change and new features.

### Downgrade

Verify the customer understands the features they will lose. Check for any minimum plan requirements. Calculate any prorated credits for the current billing cycle. Apply the new plan effective at next billing cycle unless customer requests immediate. Notify the customer of the change.

### Plan Migration

When discontinuing a plan, migrate affected customers to the closest equivalent plan. Notify customers at least 30 days before the migration. Provide comparison of old and new plan features. Offer promotional pricing if available. Assist with any device changes needed.

## Device Activation

### New Device on Existing Line

Verify the new device IMEI is compatible with the network. Check for any device locks or blacklisting. Provision the new device on the customer's existing line. Transfer any service features to the new device. Verify all services work on the new device.

### SIM Swap

Verify the customer's identity before processing. Deactivate the old SIM immediately. Activate the new SIM with the same phone number and plan. Verify service is restored on the new SIM. The old SIM becomes permanently inactive.

### eSIM Activation

Verify the device supports eSIM. Generate an eSIM QR code for the customer. Guide the customer through scanning the QR code. Verify the eSIM is activated and service is working. Deactivate any physical SIM if being replaced.

## Service Suspension

### Voluntary Suspension

Customer requests temporary service suspension. Maximum suspension period is 90 days. A reduced monthly fee applies during suspension. All services are suspended including voice, data, and messaging. Service resumes automatically after the suspension period or upon customer request.

### Involuntary Suspension

Triggered by non-payment or policy violation. Customer is notified before suspension takes effect. Suspension occurs after the grace period expires. All services are suspended. Customer must resolve the issue to restore service.

## Service Restoration

Verify the issue causing suspension is resolved. Reactivate the subscription in the billing system. Provision network access for the subscription. Verify all services are working. Notify the customer that service has been restored. Apply any applicable restoration fees.
