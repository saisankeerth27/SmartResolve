# VIP Customer Handling Procedures

## VIP Customer Identification

### VIP Tiers

Platinum: Customers with 5 or more lines and monthly spend above $500. Gold: Customers with 3-4 lines and monthly spend above $250. Silver: Customers with 2 or more lines and monthly spend above $150.

### VIP Account Indicators

The customer account is flagged with the appropriate VIP tier. VIP status is displayed in the customer service dashboard. VIP customers are routed to specialized support queues. VIP customers have dedicated account managers.

## VIP Service Standards

### Response Time

Platinum: Target 30 seconds for phone, 15 seconds for chat. Gold: Target 45 seconds for phone, 20 seconds for chat. Silver: Target 60 seconds for phone, 30 seconds for chat.

### Resolution Time

Platinum: Target 2 hours for all issues. Gold: Target 4 hours for all issues. Silver: Target 8 hours for all issues.

### Escalation Priority

VIP customers receive immediate escalation when requested. VIP issues are prioritized over standard issues. VIP customers have direct access to Level 3 support.

## VIP Support Procedures

### Dedicated Support Line

VIP customers have a dedicated phone number for support. The dedicated line has shorter wait times. Agents on the dedicated line are specially trained for VIP service.

### Account Manager Assignment

Platinum and Gold customers are assigned a dedicated account manager. The account manager is the primary point of contact for all issues. The account manager proactively reaches out for service updates and new offerings.

### Proactive Monitoring

VIP customer accounts are monitored for issues. Service quality alerts for VIP customers are prioritized. Potential issues are identified and resolved before the customer notices.

## VIP Complaint Handling

### Immediate Response

VIP complaints are acknowledged within 15 minutes. A senior agent or manager is assigned to handle the complaint. The customer receives a direct callback from the assigned handler.

### Resolution Priority

VIP complaints are resolved as the highest priority. Resources are allocated to resolve VIP complaints quickly. The customer is updated on progress at least every 2 hours.

### Compensation

VIP customers receive enhanced compensation for service issues. Compensation is authorized at higher levels to ensure customer satisfaction. Compensation includes service credits, device upgrades, and plan enhancements.

## VIP Retention

### Churn Risk Detection

VIP accounts are monitored for churn risk indicators. Indicators include: reduced usage, multiple complaints, competitor offers, and billing disputes. High-risk VIP accounts are flagged for proactive outreach.

### Retention Offers

Retention specialists are trained for VIP retention. Retention offers are tailored to the customer's specific needs. Offers may include: plan discounts, device upgrades, additional services, and loyalty rewards.

### Win-Back

VIP customers who leave receive priority treatment for win-back. Win-back offers are more generous than standard offers. The account manager is involved in the win-back process. Win-back attempts are documented and tracked.
