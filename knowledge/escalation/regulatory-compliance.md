# Regulatory Compliance Guide

## Overview

SmartConnect is subject to central and state telecommunications regulations. All employees must understand and comply with these regulations. This document provides a summary of key compliance requirements.

## TRAI Regulations

### Truth in Billing

SmartConnect must provide clear and accurate billing information. Bills must include all charges, fees, and taxes. Bills must explain any changes from the previous billing cycle. Customers must be notified of any fee increases at least 30 days in advance.

### Number Portability

Customers have the right to port their phone number to another carrier. SmartConnect must facilitate porting within 4 business days as mandated by TRAI. SmartConnect cannot refuse to port a number for any reason except past due balance. Early termination fees still apply when porting out.

### Network Disclosure

SmartConnect must disclose network management practices. Net neutrality rules must be followed. Throttling practices must be disclosed. Paid prioritization is not permitted.

### Accessibility

SmartConnect must provide accessible services for customers with disabilities. TTY/RTT services must be available. Real-time text must be supported. Video relay services must be supported. Customer service must be accessible.

## State Regulations

### Service Quality Standards

State telecom regulatory authorities may set service quality standards. SmartConnect must meet these standards to maintain its operating license. Standards include network availability, call completion rates, and customer complaint resolution times.

### Consumer Protection

State consumer protection laws apply to telecommunications services. SmartConnect must comply with unfair and deceptive practice regulations. Customers must be able to cancel service without unreasonable barriers. Refund policies must be clearly disclosed.

### Privacy Laws

The Information Technology Act, 2000 and subsequent amendments govern the collection and use of customer data. SmartConnect must obtain consent for certain data collection practices. Customers have the right to access and correct their personal data. Data breach notification requirements must be followed.

## Compliance Procedures

### Training

All employees receive compliance training upon hire and annually thereafter. Training covers TRAI regulations, state regulations, and SmartConnect policies. Training completion is tracked and documented. Employees who fail to complete training may be subject to disciplinary action.

### Audits

SmartConnect conducts regular compliance audits. Audits review billing practices, customer service interactions, and network operations. Audit findings are documented and remediated. Audit results are reported to senior management.

### Complaint Handling

Customer complaints about regulatory violations are tracked and reported. Complaints are investigated within 30 days. Complaints that result in regulatory action are escalated to the legal department. Complaint trends are analyzed for systemic issues.

## Violations and Penalties

### TRAI Penalties

TRAI violations can result in fines up to ₹10,00,000 per violation per day. Repeat violations may result in license revocation. SmartConnect maintains insurance to cover regulatory fines. Violations are reported to the board of directors.

### State Penalties

State penalties vary by jurisdiction. Fines, license suspension, and other remedies may apply. SmartConnect works with state regulators to resolve violations. Compliance improvements are implemented promptly after violations.

### Employee Violations

Employee violations of compliance policies may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees must report suspected compliance violations to the compliance department. Retaliation against employees who report violations is prohibited.
