# Device Compatibility Guide

## Network Band Requirements

### LTE Bands

SmartConnect uses the following LTE bands. Devices must support at least one band for basic service. Devices supporting multiple bands will have better coverage and performance. Required bands for full LTE experience: Band 2 (1900 MHz), Band 4 (1700/2100 MHz), Band 12 (700 MHz), and Band 66 (1700/2100 MHz).

### 5G Bands

For 5G NR service, devices must support 5G NR bands. Supported 5G bands include: n41 (2500 MHz) for mid-band 5G, n71 (600 MHz) for low-band 5G, and n260 (39 GHz) for mmWave 5G. 5G service requires a 5G-capable device and 5G plan.

### VoLTE Requirements

For voice service, devices must support Voice over LTE (VoLTE). All smartphones sold by SmartConnect are VoLTE capable. BYOD devices must be VoLTE compatible. Devices without VoLTE will not be able to make or receive calls on the SmartConnect network.

## BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) Compatibility

### Compatibility Checker

Customers can check device compatibility on the SmartConnect website. The checker uses the device IMEI to verify band support. Customers should check compatibility before purchasing a device for use on SmartConnect.

### IMEI Requirements

The device IMEI must not be blacklisted. The IMEI must be registered with the GSMA database. Devices reported as lost or stolen will not be activated. Customers can check IMEI status on the SmartConnect website.

### Device Unlocking

Devices purchased from other carriers may need to be unlocked. SmartConnect will unlock devices that are fully paid off and not reported as lost or stolen. The unlocking process takes 2-5 business days. Customers can request unlocking through customer service.

## Device Setup

### New Device Setup

When setting up a new device on SmartConnect: insert the SIM card or activate eSIM, follow the device setup wizard, connect to Wi-Fi for initial updates, configure APN settings if data does not work, and test voice, data, and messaging.

### eSIM Setup

For devices with eSIM support: scan the QR code provided by SmartConnect, follow the on-screen prompts to activate the eSIM, verify the eSIM is active in device settings, and remove the physical SIM if replacing.

### Data Transfer

When transferring from an old device to a new device: back up data from the old device, restore data on the new device, verify all apps and data transferred correctly, and test all services on the new device.

## Known Device Issues

### iPhone Issues

Some older iPhone models may have VoLTE compatibility issues. Ensure the device is running the latest iOS version. If VoLTE does not work, try toggling VoLTE off and on in Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data Options > Voice & Data.

### Android Issues

Some Android devices may require manual APN configuration. If data does not work, verify APN settings match the SmartConnect defaults. Some Samsung devices have a separate APN for T-Mobile roaming, which may conflict with SmartConnect settings.

### Pixel Issues

Google Pixel devices generally work well with SmartConnect. Ensure the device is running the latest Android version. If VoWiFi does not work, check that the device software is up to date.

## Device Care

### Battery Health

SmartConnect recommends keeping device battery above 20% for optimal performance. Extreme temperatures can affect battery life. Avoid charging the device in direct sunlight or extreme cold.

### Software Updates

Keep device software up to date for best performance and security. Software updates may include carrier settings updates that improve network performance. SmartConnect recommends enabling automatic software updates.
