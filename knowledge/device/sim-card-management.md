# SIM Card Management Guide

## SIM Card Types

### Physical SIM (nano-SIM)

Standard nano-SIM card for most smartphones. Provides voice, data, and messaging service. The SIM card is tied to the customer's account and phone number. Physical SIMs can be swapped between compatible devices.

### eSIM (embedded SIM)

Software-based SIM built into newer devices. No physical card required. Can be activated remotely by scanning a QR code. Supports multiple lines on a single device. eSIMs are more secure and cannot be physically removed.

### Micro-SIM and Standard SIM

Older SIM card formats that are no longer supported by current SmartConnect plans. Customers with these SIMs should upgrade to a nano-SIM or eSIM. SmartConnect provides free SIM upgrades upon request.

## SIM Card Provisioning

### New Line Activation

A new SIM card is generated and assigned to the customer's account. The SIM is provisioned with the customer's phone number, plan, and service features. The SIM is shipped to the customer or provided at a retail location. Activation occurs when the SIM is inserted into a compatible device.

### Replacement SIM

If a SIM card is lost, damaged, or malfunctioning, a replacement can be issued. The replacement SIM is assigned the same phone number and plan. The old SIM is deactivated immediately upon replacement. The customer must verify their identity before a replacement is issued.

### SIM Swap

A SIM swap transfers the customer's phone number and plan to a new SIM. This is different from a replacement, as it may involve a different SIM type. SIM swaps require identity verification. The old SIM is deactivated immediately.

## SIM Card Troubleshooting

### SIM Not Recognized

If the device does not recognize the SIM card: remove and reinsert the SIM card, clean the SIM card contacts with a soft cloth, try the SIM in a different device, check for physical damage to the SIM card, and contact SmartConnect for a replacement if the SIM is damaged.

### SIM Locked (PUK Code)

If the SIM is locked due to incorrect PIN entry: the PUK code is printed on the SIM card packaging, enter the PUK code when prompted on the device, set a new PIN after unlocking, and if the PUK code is lost, contact SmartConnect for assistance.

### SIM Not Activated

If a new or replacement SIM is not activating: verify the SIM has been provisioned in the SmartConnect system, insert the SIM into a compatible device and connect to Wi-Fi, wait 15 minutes for activation to complete, and contact SmartConnect if activation fails after 30 minutes.

## SIM Security

### SIM Cloning Protection

SmartConnect uses advanced encryption to prevent SIM cloning. If a SIM is suspected to be cloned, contact SmartConnect immediately. The compromised SIM will be deactivated and a replacement issued.

### SIM Lock

SIM lock prevents the use of a stolen SIM card. If a device is reported lost or stolen, the SIM is automatically locked. The customer must contact SmartConnect to unlock or replace the SIM. A locked SIM cannot make calls, send texts, or use data.

### PUK Code Security

The PUK code should be kept in a safe place. If the PUK code is entered incorrectly 10 times, the SIM is permanently locked. A permanently locked SIM requires replacement. The PUK code cannot be reset by the customer.
