# Device Financing Program

## Overview

SmartConnect offers device financing to allow customers to purchase new devices with monthly installments instead of paying the full retail price upfront.

## Financing Terms

### Payment Structure

The device cost is divided into equal monthly installments over 24 or 36 months. The installment amount is based on the device retail price and the financing term. There is no interest charged on the financing. The total cost equals the retail price of the device.

### Eligibility

Customers must have an active SmartConnect account in good standing. The account must not have any past due balance. The customer must pass a credit check for financing amounts above ₹4,999. New customers may be required to make a down payment.

### Credit Check

A soft credit check is performed for financing amounts up to ₹4,999. A hard credit check is performed for financing amounts above ₹4,999. The credit check does not affect the customer's credit score for soft checks. Customers who do not qualify for financing may pay the full retail price or make a down payment.

## Financing Agreement

### Terms and Conditions

The financing agreement is a separate contract from the service agreement. The agreement specifies the device model, retail price, installment amount, financing term, and payment due date. The agreement also specifies early termination and default provisions.

### Monthly Charges

The device installment is added to the customer's monthly service bill. The installment is a separate line item from the service charges. The installment is due on the same date as the service charges. Late payments on the installment follow the same late payment policy as service charges.

### Ownership

The customer does not own the device until all installments are paid. SmartConnect retains ownership of the device until the financing is complete. The customer is responsible for the device's care and condition. The device may not be sold or transferred while under financing.

## Early Payoff

### Full Payoff

Customers can pay off the remaining balance at any time. The payoff amount is the total of all remaining installments. No additional fees or penalties apply for early payoff. After payoff, the customer owns the device outright.

### Partial Payoff

Customers can make additional payments toward the device balance at any time. Additional payments are applied to the remaining balance. The monthly installment amount does not change with additional payments. The number of remaining installments is reduced.

## Device Return and Exchange

### Return Policy

Customers can return a financed device within 14 days of purchase. The device must be in good condition with no damage. The customer receives a refund of all installments paid minus any usage charges. The financing agreement is canceled upon return.

### Device Exchange

Customers can exchange a financed device for a different model within 30 days. The original device must be returned in good condition. The financing is transferred to the new device. Any price difference is adjusted in the monthly installment.

### Trade-In Program

Customers with a financed device can trade in for a new device. The trade-in value is applied to the new device purchase. The remaining balance on the old device is paid off from the trade-in value. Any remaining balance on the old device is added to the new financing.

## Default and Collections

### Default

A financing agreement is in default if: the customer's service is suspended for non-payment, the customer fails to make payments for 60 days, or the customer breaches the financing agreement terms. Default triggers the remaining balance becoming immediately due.

### Collections

If the financing balance remains unpaid after default, the account may be sent to collections. The customer is notified before the account is sent to collections. Collections activity may affect the customer's credit score. The customer can contact SmartConnect to arrange payment before the account is sent to collections.

## Device Insurance

### Optional Coverage

SmartConnect offers optional device insurance through a third-party provider. The insurance covers accidental damage, theft, and mechanical failure after the manufacturer warranty expires. The insurance premium is added to the monthly bill. Claims are processed through the third-party provider.

### Insurance Claims

Customers can file insurance claims through the SmartConnect app or website. A deductible applies for each claim. Approved claims result in a replacement device being shipped to the customer. Unauthorized repairs may void the insurance coverage.
