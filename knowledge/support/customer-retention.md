# Customer Retention Guide

## Overview

This document describes the strategies and procedures for retaining customers who are considering leaving SmartConnect.

## Retention Triggers

### Cancellation Request

When a customer requests to cancel service: the cancellation request triggers a retention workflow. The representative must attempt to understand the reason for cancellation. The representative must offer solutions before processing the cancellation. Retention offers may be available based on the customer's profile.

### Usage Pattern Changes

Significant decreases in usage may indicate a customer is considering leaving. Changes in usage patterns are monitored by the analytics team. Proactive outreach may be initiated for high-value customers showing churn indicators.

### Competitive Offers

Customers who mention competitive offers should be flagged for retention. The representative should highlight SmartConnect's differentiators and value. Retention offers may be available to match competitive pricing.

## Retention Strategies

### Service Resolution

The most effective retention strategy is resolving the customer's issue. If the customer is leaving due to a service problem, focus on resolving the problem. Document the issue thoroughly and follow up to ensure resolution.

### Plan Optimization

Review the customer's current plan and usage to identify optimization opportunities. The customer may be on a plan that does not match their needs. Offering a better-fitting plan can improve satisfaction and reduce cost.

### Loyalty Rewards

Long-term customers may be eligible for loyalty rewards. Loyalty rewards include plan discounts, free add-ons, and priority support. Loyalty rewards are available for customers with 2+ years of continuous service.

### Financial Incentives

Retention offers may include bill credits, plan discounts, or device offers. Retention offers are tiered based on customer value and tenure. Higher-value customers receive more generous retention offers. All retention offers must be approved by a supervisor.

## Retention Offer Matrix

### High-Value Customers

Customers spending more than $100 per month or with 3+ years of tenure. Retention offers include up to 20% plan discount for 6 months, free premium add-ons for 3 months, and waived device upgrade fees. These offers require supervisor approval.

### Medium-Value Customers

Customers spending $50-$100 per month or with 1-3 years of tenure. Retention offers include up to 10% plan discount for 3 months and free standard add-ons for 1 month. These offers require representative approval.

### Low-Value Customers

Customers spending less than $50 per month or with less than 1 year of tenure. Retention offers include one-time bill credits up to $25 and waived activation fees. These offers require representative approval.

## Cancellation Processing

### Final Attempt

Before processing a cancellation, make one final attempt to retain the customer. Summarize the value the customer receives from SmartConnect. Highlight any loyalty rewards or tenure benefits that would be lost. Offer the best available retention incentive.

### Processing Cancellation

If the customer insists on cancellation: process the cancellation effective at the end of the current billing cycle. Inform the customer of any early termination fees or device financing balances. Provide instructions for returning any rented or financed equipment. Confirm the cancellation and provide a cancellation confirmation number.

### Post-Cancellation

After cancellation: send a confirmation email with the cancellation details. Include information about any final charges or credits. Provide information about how to reactivate service if the customer changes their mind. Note the cancellation reason for analytics purposes.

## Win-Back Program

### Eligibility

Former customers who canceled within the last 90 days are eligible for the win-back program. The win-back offer is communicated via email and SMS. Win-back offers include enhanced plan pricing and waived activation fees.

### Win-Back Process

Former customers can reactivate service through the win-back link in the communication. The reactivation process is simplified for returning customers. Previous account information is used to expedite setup. Win-back customers receive priority support for the first 90 days.
