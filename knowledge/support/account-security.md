# Account Security Procedures

## Identity Verification

### Verification Methods

Customer identity must be verified before any account changes. Verification methods include: account PIN, last four digits of SSN, security question answers, email verification, and SMS verification code. Multiple methods may be required for high-risk changes.

### High-Risk Changes

The following changes require enhanced verification: account PIN change, authorized user addition or removal, plan change, device upgrade, port-out request, and account closure. Enhanced verification requires two or more verification methods.

### Verification Failure

If identity verification fails, the request is denied. The customer is advised to contact SmartConnect through an alternative channel. Account lockout occurs after 3 failed verification attempts. The customer must visit a retail location with photo ID to unlock.

## Account PIN

### PIN Requirements

The account PIN is a 4-6 digit numeric code. The PIN is required for account changes and support interactions. The PIN should not be easily guessable. The PIN should be different from the device unlock PIN.

### PIN Management

Customers set their PIN during account creation. The PIN can be changed through the customer portal, mobile app, or by contacting customer service. The PIN is not visible to SmartConnect employees. The PIN is stored securely and cannot be recovered, only reset.

### PIN Reset

If a customer forgets their PIN, they can reset it through the customer portal using email or SMS verification. If the customer cannot access the portal, they must visit a retail location with photo ID. A temporary PIN is generated and must be changed on first use.

## Account Takeover Prevention

### Suspicious Activity Detection

SmartConnect monitors accounts for suspicious activity. Indicators include: unusual login locations, rapid account changes, multiple failed verification attempts, and unusual usage patterns. Suspicious activity triggers account security alerts.

### Account Lock

If suspicious activity is detected, the account may be temporarily locked. The customer is notified via all registered contact methods. The customer must verify their identity to unlock the account. For high-risk situations, the customer must visit a retail location.

### Recovery

If an account is compromised, SmartConnect works with the customer to secure the account. This may include: resetting all passwords and PINs, reviewing recent account changes, reverting unauthorized changes, and enabling additional security features. The customer is monitored for 30 days after recovery.

## Device Security

### Device Lock

Customers should enable device lock with a PIN, password, or biometric authentication. Device lock prevents unauthorized access if the device is lost or stolen. SmartConnect recommends using at least a 6-digit PIN or alphanumeric password.

### Remote Lock and Wipe

If a device is lost or stolen, the customer can remotely lock or wipe the device. Remote lock and wipe are available through the customer portal or mobile app. Remote wipe erases all data on the device. The device cannot be used after a remote wipe without the account credentials.

### Find My Device

SmartConnect supports the Find My Device feature for compatible devices. Find My Device allows the customer to locate, lock, or wipe the device remotely. The feature requires the device to be powered on and connected to a network. Find My Device is available through the customer portal and mobile app.

## Data Privacy

### Customer Data Protection

SmartConnect protects customer data in accordance with applicable privacy laws. Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to customer data is logged and monitored. Customer data is not shared with third parties without customer consent.

### Data Retention

Customer data is retained for the duration of the service relationship. Account data is retained for 7 years after account closure. Call recordings are retained for 90 days. Support ticket records are retained for 5 years.

### Data Access Requests

Customers can request access to their personal data through the customer portal. Data access requests are processed within 30 days. Customers can also request deletion of their personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
