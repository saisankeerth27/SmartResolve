# Customer Service Standards

## Overview

This document defines the service standards and response time requirements for SmartConnect customer support operations.

## Response Time Requirements

### Phone Support

Initial connection to a representative: within 2 minutes during business hours, within 5 minutes during off-hours. Average handle time: 8-12 minutes for standard inquiries, 15-25 minutes for complex issues. Customer satisfaction target: 90% or higher.

### Chat Support

Initial response: within 30 seconds. Average resolution time: 10-15 minutes for standard inquiries. Chat support is available 24/7. Customer satisfaction target: 85% or higher.

### Email Support

Initial acknowledgment: within 4 hours during business days. Resolution: within 24 hours for standard inquiries, within 48 hours for complex issues. Email support is available for non-urgent issues. Customer satisfaction target: 80% or higher.

### Social Media Support

Initial response: within 2 hours during business hours. Resolution: within 24 hours for standard inquiries. Social media support is available for public inquiries. Customer satisfaction target: 85% or higher.

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

### Customer Response SLA

All customer inquiries must receive an initial response within the specified timeframe. The initial response must include a case number and estimated resolution time. If resolution is delayed, the customer must be updated within the originally estimated timeframe.

### Resolution SLA

Standard inquiries must be resolved within 24 hours. Complex issues must be resolved within 48 hours. Issues requiring network investigation must be resolved within 72 hours. Issues requiring vendor coordination must be resolved within 5 business days.

### Escalation SLA

If an issue cannot be resolved by the first representative, it must be escalated within 15 minutes. The escalation must include all relevant information gathered during the initial contact. The customer must be notified of the escalation and provided with the new case owner.

## Quality Standards

### First Contact Resolution

The target first contact resolution rate is 75%. First contact resolution means the issue is fully resolved during the initial interaction without requiring follow-up. Issues requiring investigation or escalation are not counted against first contact resolution.

### Customer Satisfaction

The target customer satisfaction score is 4.2 out of 5.0. Satisfaction is measured through post-interaction surveys. Surveys are sent via SMS and email. Low satisfaction scores trigger a quality review.

### Accuracy

All information provided to customers must be accurate and complete. Representatives must verify information before providing it to customers. Incorrect information must be corrected immediately. Accuracy is measured through quality audits.

## Communication Standards

### Professional Tone

Representatives must maintain a professional and courteous tone at all times. Customers must be addressed by their preferred name. Technical jargon must be avoided when explaining issues to customers. All communication must be clear and concise.

### Active Listening

Representatives must listen to the customer's full description of the issue before responding. Clarifying questions should be asked to ensure understanding. The representative must summarize the issue back to the customer for confirmation.

### Follow-Through

All promises made to customers must be fulfilled. If a callback is promised, it must be made within the specified timeframe. If information is promised, it must be provided within the specified timeframe. Failure to follow through must be documented and addressed.

## Training Requirements

### New Hire Training

All new representatives complete a 4-week training program. Training covers product knowledge, systems training, communication skills, and de-escalation techniques. Representatives must pass a certification exam before handling live interactions.

### Ongoing Training

Representatives complete 8 hours of training per quarter. Training covers new products, policy updates, and skill development. Training completion is tracked and reported. Failure to complete required training may result in performance action.
