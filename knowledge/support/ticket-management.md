# Ticket Management Procedures

## Ticket Lifecycle

### Creation

Tickets are created when a customer contacts SmartConnect with an issue. Tickets can be created via phone, chat, email, or self-service. Each ticket is assigned a unique ticket number. The ticket includes the customer's information, issue description, and initial assessment.

### Assignment

Tickets are automatically assigned to the appropriate team based on category and priority. If a specific agent is available, the ticket is assigned to that agent. If no agent is available, the ticket enters the queue for the next available agent. Customers can request a specific agent for follow-up.

### Investigation

The assigned agent investigates the issue. This may involve reviewing account information, checking network status, running diagnostics, or consulting knowledge base articles. The agent documents all findings in the ticket. If the issue requires additional expertise, the ticket is escalated.

### Resolution

Once the issue is resolved, the agent documents the resolution in the ticket. The customer is notified of the resolution. The customer confirms the issue is resolved. If the customer does not confirm within 48 hours, the ticket is automatically closed with a note that the customer did not confirm.

### Closure

Tickets are closed after the customer confirms resolution or after 48 hours without customer response. Closed tickets are archived for future reference. Customers can reopen a closed ticket within 30 days if the issue recurs.

## Ticket Priority

### Critical (P1)

Service completely unavailable. Affects business operations. No workaround available. Requires immediate attention. Response within 15 minutes. Resolution within 4 hours.

### High (P2)

Service significantly degraded. Major feature unavailable. Workaround may be available. Requires urgent attention. Response within 30 minutes. Resolution within 8 hours.

### Medium (P3)

Service partially affected. Minor feature unavailable. Workaround available. Requires attention. Response within 2 hours. Resolution within 24 hours.

### Low (P4)

Cosmetic issue. Enhancement request. Informational inquiry. Requires response. Response within 8 hours. Resolution within 72 hours.

## Ticket Categories

### Technical Support

Issues related to service functionality. Includes connectivity problems, device issues, and network problems. Assigned to the technical support team.

### Billing Support

Issues related to charges, payments, and account balance. Includes disputes, payment processing, and plan changes. Assigned to the billing support team.

### Account Management

Issues related to account setup, changes, and inquiries. Includes activation, cancellation, and account updates. Assigned to the account management team.

### Sales

New service inquiries, plan recommendations, and device purchases. Assigned to the sales team.

## Ticket Communication

### Customer Updates

Customers are notified of ticket status changes via their preferred communication method. Updates include assignment, investigation progress, escalation, and resolution. Customers can request updates at any time by contacting support.

### Internal Notes

Agents add internal notes to document investigation findings and actions taken. Internal notes are not visible to the customer. Notes are used for continuity when tickets are escalated or reassigned.

### Resolution Documentation

The resolution must be documented clearly in the ticket. Include the root cause, steps taken to resolve, and any preventive measures. Documentation is used for knowledge base updates and training.
